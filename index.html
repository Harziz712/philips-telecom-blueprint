4 Critical Updates to Maximize Call Volume:

UPDATE #1 CPC Bidding — Maximize Then Switch

Start at "Maximize clicks" for the first week. After 1 week (once learning mode finishes), go to Settings → Bidding → Change bidding strategy → "Manual CPC". Then check "Help Increase conversions with Enhanced CPC". Click Save. It should now show "CPC (enhanced)".

⚠ Do NOT enable Enhanced CPC before 1 week. Doing so prematurely will hurt your optimization score and campaign performance.

UPDATE #2 Target the Full U.S. — Not Just Zip Codes

Open your targeting to "United States" under Locations. This massively increases call volume. Pitch DirecTV + Hughes Internet first since it's available everywhere. Use BroadBandNow.com for zip-code-specific fallback providers.

UPDATE #3 Run Both Ad Types
  • "Call ad" — targets mobile phones
  • "Responsive search ad" — targets laptops, tablets & computers

Use the same verbiage for both. Running both maximizes your reach across all devices. Responsive search ads generate a TON of traffic — don't skip them.

UPDATE #4 Accept Ad Call & Messaging Terms — CRITICAL

After your ads say Eligible (a few hours after approval), look for the Terms & Conditions buttons. Click Settings on the left. If buttons aren't visible, click "SWITCH VIEW". Accept both Ad Call terms and Messaging terms in both locations.

⚠ Without accepting both, you will NOT receive phone calls or lead forms. This is the most important update.
